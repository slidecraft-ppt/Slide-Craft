Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to the questions we get asked most often — so you can order with confidence.

How long does it take to complete a project? +
Presentations are usually delivered within24–48,hours. Arduino project timelines depend on complexity and the availability of required components. For reports, documentation, or code-only work, please provide complete project details and requirements. Contact us with your deadline, and we'll provide an estimated delivery timeframe.
Do you offer revisions if I'm not happy? +
Absolutely. Every order includes free revisions until you're satisfied with the result. We want you to love the final output, not just accept it. Changes to content, layout, colors, or style are all welcome. Just share your feedback via WhatsApp and we'll turn it around quickly.
What do you need from me to get started? +
Just the basics — your topic, key points or notes, and any reference material (PDFs, images, data, rough slides). For Arduino projects, tell us the module name or problem statement. The more context you share, the better the output. No design experience needed on your end — that's exactly what we're here for.
What formats will I receive my files in? +
Presentations are delivered as editable .pptx files plus a PDF export. Reports come as both .docx and PDF. Arduino projects include source code (.ino), circuit diagrams, and a component list. All files are sent directly via WhatsApp or Google Drive — whichever you prefer.
How do I pay, and is it safe? +
Payment is accepted via UPI, Google Pay, or PhonePe — fast and familiar. For new clients, a small advance secures your slot; the remainder is due on delivery. We've completed 240+ projects with a 98% satisfaction rate, so your trust is something we take seriously.
